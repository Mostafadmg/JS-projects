CKS Pneumonia Prescribing Guidelines

Low Severity Community‑Acquired Pneumonia

  • First-line: amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily for 5 days.
  • If penicillin allergy or atypical features, use doxycycline 200 mg on day 1, then 100 mg daily for 5 days, or clarithromycin 500 mg twice daily for 5 days.

Moderate Severity Community‑Acquired Pneumonia

  • First-line: amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily plus clarithromycin 500 mg twice daily, both for 5 days.
  • If penicillin allergy, use doxycycline 200 mg on day 1, then 100 mg daily for 5 days.

Severe or Requiring Hospitalisation

  • Follow local guidelines and consider hospital referral.
